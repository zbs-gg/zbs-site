01

Show the engine thinking. Don't sell it.

zbs.gg is an independent research lab's face. Trust in the first second: hand-built by someone who knows the work — not median AI chrome.

Three principles

  • show, don't sell — live explainer beats a claim
  • terminal-editorial — mono is the voice, grotesk is personality
  • one honest accent — cyan means signal, never decoration

What stays

  • Elle — ASCII-shader video in the site hero
  • interactive lab graphics — they lead the story
  • mono / terminal DNA — cleaned, not abandoned

Each stage is real code. Rule: one effect leads per surface; the rest support. Glass and glow read as slop only when generic and stacked.

Dose: hero texture or one lab stage — not every card.

Soft signal glow

Accent-only halo. Marks the one live or active thing on a screen.

Dose: once per screen. Never on body text or every button.

07

The anti-slop line — visual proof, not only prose.

If the page is only text rows and caps micro-labels, it failed this book. Imagery, multi-demo motion, and grey glass are part of the brand contract.

Do

  • lead with interactive explainer scenes (people + contexts)
  • ≥4 effect specimens when teaching motion
  • sentence-case titles; large display section numbers
  • grey frost for content; tinted glass only for signal
  • icons / illustrations on component cards
  • mono for stage chrome & data only
  • one cyan accent = live/signal

Don't

  • tiny uppercase eyebrow above a big headline (AI default)
  • ALL CAPS micro labels as card titles
  • single ASCII teaser standing in for a system
  • text-only component grids with no imagery
  • gradient on text · Inter-only · purple glass wallpaper
  • sparkles ✦, “● live” badges, emoji-as-icon
  • a second accent “for variety”
