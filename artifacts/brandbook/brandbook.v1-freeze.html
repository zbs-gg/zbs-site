01 — essence

Show the engine thinking. Don't sell it.

zbs.gg is an independent research lab's face. The brand's job is trust in the first second: it should read as hand-built by someone who knows the work — not generated. Three principles carry that.

the three principles

  • show, don't sell — a live interactive explainer beats a claim
  • terminal-editorial — mono is the voice, a grotesk display is the personality
  • one honest accent — cyan means "signal", used rarely, never as decoration

what stays (non-negotiable)

  • Elle — the ASCII-shader video in the hero. Intentional, stays.
  • the interactive explanatory graphics from the lab — they lead
  • the mono / terminal DNA — cleaned, not abandoned

03 — color

Near-black, off-white, one signal.

04 — effects lab

The three effects you like — dosed, not dumped.

ASCII animation, frosted glass, soft glow. Each is real and code-native here. The craft rule: one effect leads per surface, the others support. Glass and glow read as slop only when generic and stacked — here they sit on a terminal base and stay rare.

ascii animationyour signature. canvas + mono glyphs, low-opacity texture. the same family as Elle's shader.
backdrop-filter: blur(16px)
over a colored field
frosted glassa panel that floats over motion/color. one per view, thin light border, never over plain black.
soft glowaccent-only halo (box-shadow). marks the one live/active thing. dose: once per screen.

06 — guardrails

The anti-slop line.

do

  • lead with an interactive explainer, not a paragraph
  • mono for labels/data, grotesk for headlines
  • one cyan accent = signal; holo only in data-viz
  • real photo of the lab / person where trust is needed
  • hard thin rules, 2–4px radius, generous whitespace
  • effects dosed: one leads, others support

don't

  • gradient on text (the loudest AI tell)
  • Inter or Geist as display · Fraunces + brown italics
  • sparkles ✦, "● live" badges, emoji-as-icon
  • glassmorphism everywhere · 30%+ radius · glow on everything
  • walls of text where a diagram would say it
  • a second accent color "for variety"
